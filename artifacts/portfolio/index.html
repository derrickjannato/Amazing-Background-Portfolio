Featured project

Telco Customer Churn Prediction

A complete machine learning pipeline that predicts which telecom customers are about to leave — and explains why, so the business can act before they go.

Machine Learning · Classification

Predicting customer churn with 85% accuracy

Telecom companies lose millions every year to customer churn. Acquiring a new subscriber is 5-10× more expensive than retaining one — yet most churn happens silently before any human intervention. This project builds a supervised classification model that flags at-risk customers early, using behavior, contract, and billing signals from 7,000+ accounts.

Problem

Predict who is about to leave

Identify subscribers most likely to cancel within the next billing cycle, using contract terms, tenure, services, and monthly charges — so retention can be targeted, not blanket.

Method

End-to-end ML pipeline

  • Cleaning & encoding of 21 features (7,043 customers)
  • EDA on tenure, contract type, payment method
  • Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost
  • Cross-validation + threshold tuning
Insights & impact

What drives churn

  • Month-to-month contracts churn 4× more than 2-year
  • Fiber optic + electronic check = highest risk segment
  • First 12 months of tenure are critical
  • Targeted retention could save ~15% revenue
